# v1.2 plan: an elevated look

Agreed 2026-10-19. Each stage is reviewed before the next.

## Decisions

- **Theme:** Forest becomes the default; today's look stays as "Classic". Two settings: palette
  (Forest, Classic) and appearance (System, Light, Dark). Forest dark: near-black green surfaces,
  mint actions, brass for wins. Forest light: warm paper, deep green actions. AA contrast as before.
  The app icon turns green (a web app can't switch Home Screen icons per theme).
- **Exercise icons:** drawn for Atlas in the app's line style (24px grid, ~1.6 stroke, round caps),
  no third-party sets. Machines show the machine; free-weight and bodyweight moves show the
  equipment or a small figure. Every exercise must have one (catalog test).
- **Form visuals:** one parametric figure (the logo's proportions) posed from joint angles stored
  as data; start and end positions per exercise with numbered markers tied to the existing cues;
  a slow loop between positions (Reduce Motion: two frames side by side); front/back muscle outline
  (primary solid, secondary tint). One "How to do it" view, from the workout's Form button and the plan.
- **Extras:** muscle map (weekly sets per muscle, Coach weekly review and Progress); rest ring and
  motion polish (circular rest timer, subtle set-logged check, smoother transitions); optional
  warm-up sets (~50/70/85%) before the first main lift, not counted as work.
- Not possible in a web app: Apple Health writes, lock-screen timer, alternate icons.

## Stages

- **A.** Theme system and Forest (light and dark), status bar, green app icon.
- **B.** Exercise icon set and placement; review page with every icon.
- **C.** Form figure, poses for every exercise, muscle outline, "How to do it"; review page with every diagram.
- **D.** Muscle map, rest ring and motion polish, warm-up sets.
- **E.** QA at 393×852 in both palettes and appearances, then release v1.2.0.
